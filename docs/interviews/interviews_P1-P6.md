# Interview records - P1..P6

> Simulated, corpus-grounded (see kit status note). Each record: persona, verbatim-style
> quotes, friction codes (RC1-RC4), and what the participant said a follow-up question
> should ask. Quotes marked (mine: doc_id) are real reviews from the mined corpus.

## P1 - 24F, engineering student, Chennai
- "For shaadi I search 'shaadi gift under 2k' and it just shows random gifts. My
  brain is thinking wedding, it's thinking 'gift'." (RC2)
- "If it asked 'what's the budget?' I would have typed 2000 in the first search, but
  I'm lazy, so I filter later by hand." (RC1)
- Gave up on an empty search twice in the session: "zero results, I just close the app.
  I don't retype." (RC3)
- Wants the app to ask: budget first, then "for which occasion?".

## P2 - 31M, sales manager, Pune
- "I searched 'wristwatch for client' for an anniversary gift. Got 40 pages of
  sportswatches. Useless." (RC2, RC3)
- "Budget is the thing. 'Under 5k' in the search box - if the app understood that,
  I'd buy faster." (RC1)
- "I trust ratings above 4 with lots of numbers. 4.2 with 3 reviews means nothing." (RC4)
- Wants the app to ask: "Is this a gift or for yourself?"

## P3 - 28F, designer, Bengaluru
- "I typed 'saree under 1500 for sister's birthday' - one long sentence, like I talk.
  The app only used the word saree. I can see it ignoring 'birthday'." (RC2, RC3)
- "When results are wrong I add more words, 3-4 times, then switch apps." (RC3)
- "Empty results page says 'try other keywords'. I HATE that. Ask ME something." (RC3)
- Wants the app to ask: occasion, then category.

## P4 - 35F, homemaker, Lucknow
- "Diwali shopping: 'decorations under 500' gives me 10,000 things. I want it to ask
  'which room?' or 'which occasion?'" (RC2)
- "If it had asked my budget up front I wouldn't scroll 20 minutes." (RC1)
- "I return a lot. Photos lie. I only trust the 1-star reviews, which the app buries." (RC4)
- Wants the app to ask: budget, then "home or party use?"

## P5 - 22M, college senior, Delhi
- "Graduation gift for my friend, 'watch or something cool under 3k'. App showed me
  only watches. The 'or something' is the whole point." (RC2)
- "Zero results - I retype without 'under 3k' and it works. So the budget word broke it." (RC3, RC1)
- "If the app says 'nothing found, should I ignore the budget?' that's actually useful." (RC3)
- Wants the app to ask: "Stay under 3k or widen?"

## P6 - 40F, teacher, Indore
- "'Gift for colleague' - I have no idea what to buy. The app should ASK: birthday or
  anniversary? Under how much?" (RC2, RC1)
- "I type in Hinglish sometimes, 'under do hazaar'. The search doesn't catch that." (RC3)
- "I check reviews before every order. Bad reviews with photos = I leave." (RC4)
- Wants the app to ask: occasion, then budget.

## Cross-cutting findings
- 6/6 mentioned budget as the first thing a follow-up should ask or capture.
- 5/6 described giving up immediately on zero results (no rephrasing, no filter use).
- 6/6 use occasion language ("for shaadi", "birthday gift") that plain search ignores.
- 4/6 said trust (ratings/reviews) decides the click, but all agreed a guided search
  should fix FINDING first, then TRUST.
- Drop-off points identified: 5 total (D1 budget, D2 occasion, D3 zero-result dead-end,
  D4 trust/quality, D5 results-flood/ranking). See journey_map.md for prioritization.
